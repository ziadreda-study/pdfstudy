What is this site?

Mothakertak is a free tool that runs straight in your browser, with no downloads and no account. It opens PDF, PowerPoint (pptx), Word (docx), text files and images, and gives you tools to study with: a highlighter for what matters, a pen for drawing and scribbling, text on the page, an eraser, and translation to Arabic. When you finish, you save a new copy of the file with all your edits on it.

The most important feature: your file never leaves your device. Everything happens inside your own browser, so your study files stay private to you.

Who is it for?

For any student who studies from digital files: university lectures, summaries, PDF books, or lecture slides. Instead of printing paper or switching between programs, everything a student needs is on one page.
