Your files belong to you alone

The Mothakertak app runs entirely inside the browser on your device. The files you open (PDF, PowerPoint, Word or images) are never uploaded to any server, and we never see or store them. All the tools (highlighting, drawing and writing) work locally on your device only, and when you close the page no copy of your file remains with us.

The data this site collects

On our side we collect no data about visitors automatically: no account registration and no tracking from us.

The only thing that reaches us is the "contact us" form: if you choose to contact us, the form collects only what you type (your name if you enter it, your email, and your message text), and it is delivered to the site owner so they can reply. These messages are never published, sold, or shared with any third party other than the hosting service that delivers the form (Netlify), and they are never used for ads.

Google ads (Google AdSense)

This site uses Google AdSense ads to cover its costs, and Google, as a third party, uses technologies like cookies to show suitable ads:

  • Google and its advertising partners may use cookies (including DoubleClick cookies) to show ads based on your previous visits to different sites.
  • You can turn off personalized ads at any time from Google's ad settings.
  • For more details on how Google uses data when you visit sites that use its services, see Google's official page.

Optional tools that use external services

Most of the app works on your device without sending anything, but two optional tools send the text you choose to an external service when you use them:

  • Translation: when you select a word or sentence and ask for a translation, only the selected text is sent to a translation service.
  • Ask Chat (AI assistant): when you ask a question, your question text is sent to a third-party AI service so it can answer.

If you never use these two tools, no text from your file ever leaves your device. And your file itself is never sent in any case - only the selected text is sent, at the moment you use the tool.

Cookies in general

Cookies are small files the browser stores. Most browsers give you full control: you can delete them or block them from the browser settings. If you block them, the study tools themselves will keep working normally.

External links

The site may contain links to other sites (like the Google pages mentioned above). We are not responsible for the privacy policies of those sites, so we recommend reading them when you visit.

Changes to this policy

If anything changes in how we handle privacy, we will update this page and change the "last updated" date above.
