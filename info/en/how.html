How to study with the app

Six simple steps from opening a file to saving your final copy.

1
Open the app in your browser

On your phone or computer, go to the app link. No downloads and no sign-up, the page opens right away.

2
Pick your file

Tap the "choose a file" button and select a file from your device: PDF, PowerPoint, Word, a text file or an image. The file opens inside your browser and is never sent anywhere.

3
Move between pages

Use the "previous" and "next" arrows at the top, type a page number, or open the "pages" panel to see thumbnails and jump quickly to what you need.

4
Highlight, write and draw

From the tools menu 🛠️ pick what you need: "highlighter" for what matters, "pen" for free drawing, "text" for writing, "rectangle" for marking, and "eraser" for fixes. Every edit goes on your own copy only.

5
Translate what is unclear

If an English word or sentence confuses you, use the translation tool and see the meaning in Arabic without leaving the page.

6
Save your copy

When you finish, save a new copy of the file with all your highlights and notes. Come back to it before the exam as if you never closed your summary.

Quick tip: use one highlight color for definitions and another for examples - your final revision will be much faster. See more study tips.
